# GAPS Taxonomy

- Clients use exact labels such as C1–C5 and explicit source/target roles.
- Split labels such as `7:2:1` and `8:2` are different protocols until proven otherwise.
- DA modes such as `fixed`, `CORAL`, `MMD`, and `strong` are not interchangeable.
- Models such as `R3aK16` and `R4a` require exact version/profile identity.
- Calibration modes include `none`, `bias`, `affine`, `full`, `specialist`, and `auto_v2` only when verified.
- QC must preserve policy/version and accepted/review/reject sample scope.
- Routed, calibrated, accepted-only, reviewed, and route-correct metrics describe different populations.
