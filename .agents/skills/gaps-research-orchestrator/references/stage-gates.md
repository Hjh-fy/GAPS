# Stage Gates

1. **Planning:** hypotheses, baselines, ablations, metrics, and acceptance criteria are confirmed.
2. **Registration:** experiment IDs and provenance-critical fields are confirmed.
3. **Execution:** runs occur outside these Skills and yield traceable results.
4. **Analysis:** confirmed schemas have statistical summaries and uncertainty.
5. **Audit:** comparison fairness, completeness, reproducibility, and leakage checks pass.
6. **Evidence:** approved Evidence maps to scoped claims.
7. **Writing:** existing `research-writing-skill` drafts only from approved inputs.
8. **Number audit:** manuscript occurrences match canonical approved values.

A stage is current until its exit gate is satisfied. A later artifact does not waive an earlier blocking gate.
