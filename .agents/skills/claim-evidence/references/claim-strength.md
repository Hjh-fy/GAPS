# Claim Strength

- `direct`: audited Evidence tests the same population, intervention, comparison, outcome, and scope.
- `qualified`: direct Evidence supports a narrower claim with an explicit condition.
- `indirect`: Evidence is related but cannot establish the stated causal or general claim.
- `contradictory`: reliable Evidence conflicts with the claim.
- `missing`: no traceable Evidence supports the claim.

Use cautious wording for indirect or qualified support. “Significant” requires an appropriate statistical result, not visual separation.
