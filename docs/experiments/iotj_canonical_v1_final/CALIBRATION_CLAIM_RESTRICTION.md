# Calibration-claim restriction

Status: `CLAIM_RESTRICTED`.

Canonical-v1 uses a predefined, role-aware commissioning calibration split of approximately 20% for each target device (C3: 678 windows; C4: 320; C5: 320). No canonical calibration-budget sensitivity study has been executed, so the evidence supports only the factual wording **“a predefined 20% commissioning calibration set.”**

The manuscript must not describe canonical-v1 as few-shot, limited-calibration, small-budget, low-calibration, or sample-efficient commissioning. No conclusion may be drawn about 10%, 25%, or 50% calibration budgets. A future backend-only R84/QC budget sensitivity would not by itself establish end-to-end adaptation-budget sensitivity.

This restriction avoids a new experiment because the planned manuscript claim does not require a calibration-budget comparison.
